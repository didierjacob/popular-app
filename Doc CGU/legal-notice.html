Article 7.Reporting illicit content

In accordance with Article 6.I.5 of French Law no. 2004-575 of 21 June 2004 on confidence in the digital economy (Loi pour la Confiance dans l'Économie Numérique — LCEN) and Article 16 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 on digital services (Digital Services Act), any user may report to the Publisher:

  • Manifestly illicit content (hatred, discrimination, infringement of privacy, defamation);
  • The presence of a public figure whose inclusion is contested (private person, minor, removal request);
  • Fraudulent behaviour (multi-accounts, vote manipulation);
  • Any other fact likely to require moderation action.

Reports must be sent by email to popularoo@popularoo.com, specifying:

  • The precise identification of the content or fact reported;
  • The reason for the report;
  • Where applicable, evidence allowing the illicit nature of the content to be established.

The Publisher undertakes to examine reports within a reasonable time and to take, where applicable, the appropriate measures (removal of content, suspension of the user, invalidation of fraudulent votes, reporting to the competent authorities).
