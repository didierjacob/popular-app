3. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

In accordance with the GDPR, we process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Article 6.1.b GDPR): to provide the Service, process your Booster purchases, display your public profile if you have purchased a Booster
  • Legitimate interest (Article 6.1.f GDPR): to prevent fraud and vote manipulation, ensure Service security
  • Consent (Article 6.1.a GDPR): for sending emails not strictly necessary for the service
  • Legal obligation (Article 6.1.c GDPR): to respond to competent authorities in case of legal request

4. Use of Information

  • Score calculation: Aggregate votes to calculate popularity scores
  • Rankings: Display trending public figures and tops
  • Booster visibility: Display your name and social media on the home page if you purchase a Booster
  • Transactional emails: Purchase confirmations and expiration reminders (only if you provide an email)
  • Anti-abuse: Limit votes to one per 24 hours per public figure

5. Data Storage and Security

  • On your device: Identifier, account info, voting history, badges
  • On our servers: Aggregated votes, Booster purchases, transaction history, score graphs
  • Security: HTTPS (TLS) encryption for all communications, secure cloud hosting, no payment data stored
  • Hosting: Application server hosted by Render Services, Inc. — Oregon region, United States (US West); database hosted by MongoDB Atlas, United States

International transfers: Your data may be processed on servers located in the United States. These transfers are governed by the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission and, where applicable, by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. These mechanisms ensure a level of protection equivalent to that of the European Union.

6. Data Retention

  • Voting data: Retained as long as the account is active, then anonymized in score aggregates
  • Booster account data: Retained as long as the Booster profile is active, then deleted within 12 months of deactivation
  • Transaction data: Retained for 10 years in accordance with French accounting obligations
  • Technical logs: Retained for a maximum of 12 months for security reasons

7. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your data.

  • Public display: Scores, rankings, and Booster profiles are public by design
  • Service providers (sub-processors): Hosting (MongoDB Atlas, Render.com), transactional email (Brevo) — bound by GDPR-compliant confidentiality agreements
  • Legal obligations: If required by law, a judicial or competent administrative authority

8. Your Rights

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: obtain a copy of your personal data
  • Right to rectification: correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): request deletion of your data
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability: receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to object
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time (without retroactive effect)

Check your history in "My Votes" and your data in "Account".

To exercise your rights: popularoo@proton.me (response within 30 days maximum).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the French Data Protection Authority (CNIL): www.cnil.fr. Users in other EU countries may lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority.

If you are a public figure featured on Popularoo and wish to request removal from the platform, consult Article 7 of our Terms of Service or write directly to popularoo@proton.me with the subject "Removal Request".

Admissible requests are processed within 72 business hours.

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If we discover that a minor under 16 has provided data, we will delete it immediately.

Parents or guardians noticing use of the Application by an unauthorized minor: contact us at popularoo@proton.me.
